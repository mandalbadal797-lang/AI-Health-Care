# MindCampus — Community Discussion Guidelines

## 1. Student Code of Conduct

MindCampus provides a supportive, educational environment for college students. When participating in content discussions, all students must adhere to the following rules:

1. **Be Respectful & Supportive**: Keep comments constructive, empathetic, and encouraging. Personal attacks, harassment, and hate speech are strictly prohibited.
2. **Protect Personal Privacy**: Do not post private contact information, phone numbers, email addresses, or credentials.
3. **Non-Clinical Boundary**: Do not offer medical advice, prescribe treatments, or attempt to diagnose other students.
4. **No Bullying or Unsafe Content**: Self-harm encouragement, violence, dangerous challenges, and promotional spam are prohibited and will result in comment removal.
