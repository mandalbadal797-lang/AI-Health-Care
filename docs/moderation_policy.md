# MindCampus — Platform Moderation Policy & Safety Rules

## 1. Non-Clinical Boundary Policy

MindCampus content must provide supportive, educational, and motivational guidance for college students.
1. **Zero Medical/Clinical Advice**: Content must NOT attempt to diagnose mental health conditions or prescribe clinical treatments.
2. **Medical Claims Verification**: Content containing phrases like *"X cures anxiety"* or *"100% guaranteed fix"* must be flagged for human review.

---

## 2. AI-Assisted Content Policy

1. **Traceability**: All AI-assisted content is tagged with `is_ai_generated = True` and linked to its generation ID.
2. **Equal Quality Standards**: AI content is evaluated using the same safety, readability, and factual accuracy checklists as human-authored content.
3. **Mandatory Human Approval**: AI content can NEVER automatically publish itself.
